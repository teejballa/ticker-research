<objective>
Wave 3 ships the **regime-conditional source-weight layer**: the `(source, regime)` weights that the aggregator reads PER-ROW from `SentimentSnapshot.regime`. This is the layer CORE-ML-20 + CORE-ML-21 require, and it satisfies CLAUDE.md rule #4 literally — sparse regime cells regress to the unconditional `(source, 'ALL')` weight via Beta-Binomial empirical-Bayes shrinkage (D-07 hybrid recipe).

Per D-08 + Pitfall 1: aggregator NEVER calls `classifyRegimeAt({ asOf: new Date() })`. The regime read is PIT-correct by construction — it comes from `SentimentSnapshot.regime`, which was written at scan time by Wave 1 (or historically by Wave 2). Mixed-regime aggregation windows yield per-row weights without distortion.

Per D-09 cold-start chain: `(source, regime, asOf)` → `(source, 'ALL', asOf)` → `1.0`. EB shrinkage operates ONLY between step 1 and step 2 — not step 3.

Output: extended `getWeightForSource` signature; extended source-tier-recompute cron with per-regime iteration + EB shrinkage; aggregator wiring at line 405 of `aggregator.ts` per RESEARCH §E.
</objective>
